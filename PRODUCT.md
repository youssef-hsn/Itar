# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who wants a one-off framed image and does not need design skill. They arrive with a photo (or image file), want an Arabic-bordered result quickly, and leave with a download—not an account or a project to manage.

## Product Purpose

Itar is a lightweight, client-side web tool that styles and wraps images in beautiful, customizable Arabic borders using simple parameters. The work runs entirely in the browser; the product can be hosted as frontend-only (e.g. on Cloudflare) with no backend. Success is a first-time visitor who uploads or provides an image, tweaks a few parameters, and downloads a framed result in under a minute.

## Positioning

Instant, param-driven Arabic borders—no account required.

## Brand Personality

**Calm · Precise · Fast.** The interface stays quiet and legible; the craft shows in the bordered output. Speed and clarity beat ornamented chrome.

## Anti-references

- Heavy “Islamic heritage” theme-park UI (gold everything, busy arabesque wallpaper as chrome)
- Bloated design suites (Canva / Photoshop vibes—toolbars everywhere)
- Generic AI SaaS (purple gradients, feature grids, signup walls)

## Design Principles

1. **Finish in one sitting** — the happy path ends in a download, not a signup or save dialog.
2. **Parameters over panels** — customization stays simple and legible; never a design suite.
3. **Ornament in the output, quiet in the chrome** — Arabic border craft shows in the image; the UI stays calm and precise.
4. **Trust the device** — processing stays client-side; no account, no upload-to-server story.
5. **Casual-user accessible** — AA contrast/structure, plus non-color-only state, large targets, and clear screen-reader labels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Additionally: color-blind safe controls (do not rely on hue alone for state), large touch targets for the mobile framing flow, and screen-reader friendly labels on every control and result state. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` as a sensible default alongside those requirements.
